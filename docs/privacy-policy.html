Berani Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 (Berani version 3.1). Ringkasan Bahasa Melayu di bawah.

In short: Berani has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Your cases, notes and screenshots are stored only on your phone. Your voice, text and screenshots leave your phone only when you choose to use Berani AI (telling Berani what happened, checking screenshots, or writing Borang 1), and our server does not keep a copy.

1. Who we are

Berani is a free anti bullying app for Malaysia, developed by Dr Sherman Koh. It helps students and parents record what happened, keep proof, find help, and prepare a case for a school anti bully committee or the Tribunal for Anti-bully under the Anti-bully Act 2026. Berani is not a government service, a law firm or an emergency service.

2. What stays on your phone

Everything you create in Berani is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • cases, incident descriptions, dates and places;
  • photos and screenshots you add as proof (copied into the app's private folder);
  • names and contact details you enter for your record, the message to the school and Borang 1;
  • your answers for Borang 1 (the Tribunal's complaint form), except your IC number, home address, occupation and office address, which Berani never saves.

We have no copy of this data and cannot see it. It is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall Berani. If you back up your phone (for example iCloud or Google backup), your device may include Berani's data in that backup under your own account.

How Berani uses AI

Berani uses AI for four tasks only, and you check and can change everything it produces before anything is saved or shared:

  • turning your voice recording into words (anything that does not sound like speech is dropped, so silence is never turned into words);
  • sorting what you said into a record: when, where, what happened, who did it, who saw it;
  • drafting the "facts" part of Borang 1 only from the record you confirmed;
  • describing screenshots you choose to check.

Berani has no AI chat. It does not give legal advice or decide whether a case can go to the Tribunal. For questions about the Tribunal, Berani sends you to the Tribunal's own channels: BAYU, the AI assistant of BHEUU that the Tribunal's portal uses, Tanya Kami on tab.bheuu.gov.my, and its phone line. For someone to talk to, Berani connects you to Talian Kasih, the Ministry of Education's bullying line, Befrienders and Buddy Bear. Berani sends nothing to BAYU or these services: it only opens them, and what you type there is covered by their own privacy terms.

4. Who processes it

  • Berani server, hosted by Render. It passes your request to the AI provider and returns the answer. It does not store or log the content of requests. It keeps your internet address in memory for up to one hour only to limit misuse (rate limiting).
  • AI provider: OpenAI (which also turns voice into text), or Anthropic if we switch providers. They process the request to generate the answer. Under their API terms they do not use this data to train their models, and they may keep it for a limited period (for example up to 30 days) for safety and abuse monitoring before deleting it.

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. These services may be located outside Malaysia.

5. What we do not do

  • No account or sign in.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No contacting schools, the Tribunal, the police or anyone else on your behalf. You decide what to share and with whom.

6. Permissions

  • Photos: only when you choose screenshots or photos to add as proof.
  • Camera: only when you take a photo as proof.
  • Microphone: only while you are recording in Tell Berani.
  • Phone and links: tapping a hotline opens your phone dialler or WhatsApp (with a short greeting you can change before sending, never details of your case); tapping BAYU or the Tribunal portal opens your browser.

7. Sharing Borang 1 and messages

Berani helps you report through the official channels: the school's anti-bully committee and the Tribunal's own portal, tab.bheuu.gov.my. Berani never files anything itself. When you make a Borang 1 PDF or a message to the school, Berani creates it on your phone and opens your phone's share options or email app. Where it goes is your choice. Borang 1 may identify a child. Share it only with a parent, the school, the Tribunal, the police or a lawyer. Publishing information that identifies a child in Tribunal proceedings is an offence under section 50 of the Anti-bully Act 2026.

8. Children and parents

Berani is designed for students, including those under 18, and their parents. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. We encourage young users to involve a parent, guardian or trusted adult. Parents can review and delete everything in the app on the child's phone.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use Berani without the AI features: typing what happened, proof, the school message and Borang 1 all work without them.
  • Delete any incident, screenshot or whole case in the app at any time.
  • Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you may contact us with any question about your data.

10. Safety

If you are in danger, call 999. For someone to talk to: Talian Kasih 15999 (24 hours) or WhatsApp 019-261 5999, Befrienders 03-7627 2929 (24 hours).
