Berani – Speak Up Safely

Tell Berani what happened, by voice or text. It keeps a private record and helps you report to the school and Malaysia’s Tribunal for Anti-bully, the official way. Free, private, no account.

Google Play App Store

Key features

  • Record incidents with screenshots, kept privately on your phone
  • A timeline that shows the pattern and whether it is getting worse
  • Speak in Bahasa Melayu or English; Berani writes it down for you
  • A ready message for the school, and Borang 1 filled in for the Tribunal's official portal
  • AI check of what happened, with safety advice
  • Berani Counsellor, with Talian Kasih, Befrienders and OSCC one tap away

Support • Privacy Policy

Important: Berani is not an emergency service. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number.
