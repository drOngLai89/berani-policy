Frequently asked questions

Is Berani an emergency service? No. Call 999 if you are in danger.

Where are my cases stored? Only on your phone. We have no copy. Deleting the app deletes them.

What does the AI see? Only the incident text and up to 3 screenshots you choose to analyse, and your messages to Berani Counsellor. Our server does not keep them. Names in your case file are never sent.

Can my case go to the Tribunal for Anti-bully? Use the Tribunal check in the app. For bullying at school, complain to the school's anti bully committee first. If they take no action or handle it badly, the student or a parent can file at tab.bheuu.gov.my (free). Tribunal enquiries: 03-2271 1000, tab@bheuu.gov.my.

How do I move to a new phone? Export each case file as a PDF and keep it safely. Cases do not sync between phones.

I used the old Berani. Where are my reports? Version 3 moves them into a case called "Saved reports from the old Berani".
